What you need

From Rosie

What you need on your side

I run on your own computer, through your own broker account. These are the things you need in place. Nothing here is a shortcut around your broker’s own rules.

  1. 01

    A Windows computer

    I run on Windows, on your computer, not in a browser and not on a phone. Phone and tablet access is being built and is not available yet. Lime Signalworks currently installs me with you (there is no self-serve download yet). A written “what you’ll see” guide comes with the install. The production installer is meant to be a double-click, with no administrator rights; that path is still being smoothed.

  2. 02

    Your own Schwab or Interactive Brokers account

    You use your own brokerage account. I do not replace your broker, and I do not hold your broker password. During the free month I run in practice mode, with no real-money orders. With Schwab, practice mode is a dry run on your computer: I go through the same checks and keep a record, but orders are not sent to Schwab. With Interactive Brokers, practice orders go to IB’s paper account and use real market prices.

  3. 03

    If you use Schwab: your own developer app

    Each Schwab customer needs their own Schwab developer account and app. Approval can take several days. The app’s callback address must be set to https://127.0.0.1:8182. Schwab’s rules for a paid product that uses the Schwab API are still being worked through; that review may affect when a paid Schwab setup can go live. That fact will not be dressed up as settled.

The path

From Rosie

The path, step by step

This is the path as it stands today, including the parts that are not finished.

  1. 01

    See how I work

    Read How Rosie Works, and look at the sample-data demo if you like. You stay in charge: you set the rules, and you supervise me.

  2. 02

    Write to Lime Signalworks

    Email Lime Signalworks to begin. A registration form is planned so you are on the customer list used for setup and later maintenance updates. That form is not on this page yet.

  3. 03

    Install, with a guide

    Lime Signalworks sets me up with you on your Windows computer and walks you through a “what you’ll see” guide. If anyone at Lime Signalworks ever looks in to help, you stay in charge; any remote view is view-only.

  4. 04

    The free month

    You watch me work in practice mode for one month. Every proposal, order, stop and journal entry is there for you to see. Nothing is sent as a real-money order. You weigh the month on the evidence, and you decide whether I stay.

  5. 05

    Payment, if you continue

    If you choose to continue, payment is arranged after the free month. No payment is taken on this site, and no payment processor is connected here yet.

  6. 06

    Go-live after payment

    Going live with real-money orders, after payment, is still being designed. One approach under consideration is a signed unlock code tied to your computer. That licensing is not built yet, and this page is not a finished licensing product. You remain the person who turns live trading on; I do not flip that switch for you.
