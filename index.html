What Rosie does, and the rules that hold her to it.

Rosie trades a paper account only today. We don't publish profit figures. This page shows the rules every order has to pass, how we test them, and what Rosie won't do. Options, futures, crypto and forex are COMING. None of them is live.

Five numbers, each with its source

Disaster stop

10% default

A protective stop sits at the broker on every position. It is sent with the buy order, for the same number of shares, and stays in place until the position is closed. Its price is set at entry and doesn't move. It is never set tighter than three times the stock's usual overnight gap, so jumpier stocks get more room.

Source: Rosie's written protection rules

Risk per trade

2% of equity

Each position is sized so that if its stop is hit, the loss at the stop price is at most 2% of the account. A fast market can still fill below the stop.

Source: Rosie's written sizing rules

Total open risk

6% of equity

Every order passes the same safety checks before it is sent. All open positions and pending orders together can't put more than 6% of the account at risk, about three full-size trades. Any order that would go over is refused.

Source: Rosie's written risk limits

Trailing stops

0

We took trailing stops out on 21 October 2026. Entries, partial sales and exits come from her signal rules instead, and an automated check makes sure she never creates a trailing stop.

Source: our decision of 21 Oct 2026, checked by an automated test

Rosie's rules are checked by an automated test suite. In the latest full run, on 27 October 2026, none of the tests that ran failed. A few were skipped because they need a web browser the test machine didn't have.

Our TradingView indicators

Lime Signalworks also offers its own TradingView indicators: rule-based tools that turn the chart into plain readings you can check, instead of a guess. They work on their own, on your TradingView chart. This is the one slightly technical corner of the page: each one is shown with the problem it was made to solve.

  • No. 1

    Lime Color Weather

    The problem it solves You trade the same way whether the market is friendly or stormy, and only find out which it was afterwards.

    Shows the market’s current “weather” as colour bands, so you can see whether conditions favour buying, holding or staying small.

    A little technical Conditions are drawn as colour bands with readings such as “bull cooling” or “strong bull rising”. The same read appears in more than one pane, and the middle pane shows whether a tiring move is forming with or against the larger trend.

  • No. 2

    Lime Notify

    The problem it solves There are so many signals on your screen that you can’t tell what you’re actually supposed to do.

    Reduces the noise to three readings only: Buy, Trim and Exit, each checked on the 1-day, 6-hour and 3-hour time frames.

    A little technical The published reading is Alignment + Momentum − Exhaustion − Risk: Buy when it is strong, Trim when it softens, Exit when it breaks down. Tiredness and risk count against a Buy.

  • No. 3

    Lime Exhaustion

    The problem it solves You keep getting in too late, buying right as the move runs out of steam.

    Shows whether a move is fresh, mid-journey or worn out, so you can avoid buying the last gasp of a move.

    A little technical The published reading is Stretch from Trend + Momentum Extremes + Volatility Expansion − Fresh Support. The further price stretches from its trend, the more tired the move reads.

  • No. 4

    Lime StopWatcher

    The problem it solves You never know where to put your stop, so you either get shaken out or give back what you made.

    Three tools for the life of a trade: a first stop level at entry, a trailing guard as the trade moves your way, and a line that marks where a mature move may be ending.

    A little technical Three parts: StopWatcher I (First Stop), StopWatcher II (Trailing Guard) and StopWatcher III (Harvest Line), which gives its final read as CAND, AVOID, WATCH or HOLD.

  • No. 5

    Lime 8 Filter Stack

    The problem it solves When the signals are mixed, it’s easy to talk yourself into the trade anyway.

    Combines several safety checks into one pass or fail view, so you can stand aside when the signals are mixed.

  • The indicators show their logic. They support your decisions and do not make them for you.
  • Indicators do not predict the market, and no indicator removes the risk of loss.

Section 08

What Rosie does for you

Rosie does the watching, the sizing, the order entry and the protection. You set the rules and stay in charge.

Most people cannot watch the market all day, and most people who try end up breaking their own rules under pressure. Rosie is built to follow the rules you set: no impulse buys, no oversized positions, no skipped stops.

She samples prices through the session, checks every idea against your risk limits, sizes the position, sends the order to your broker and attaches protection the moment it fills. Everything she does shows up on the Operator Workstation, where you can step in, pause her or shut everything down at any time.

Section 09

How Rosie works, in three steps

Set her up once. Let her run. Keep watch.

  1. Step one

    You set the rules

    Pick your markets, your risk per trade and your daily loss limit. We set Rosie up with you on a paper account first, so you see her work before any real money is involved.

  2. Step two

    Rosie watches and trades

    She tracks prices against their recent averages and reads the day’s stated market posture. When your rules say enter or exit, she sizes the position and places the order. On a defensive day, new buys are blocked.

  3. Step three

    You supervise

    Every position, stop and order is on your screen, and every decision is written to a Journal and Trade Record you keep. Live trading runs only when you switch it on, and one click stops it all.

Section 10

Built to protect the account first

Before Rosie chases a gain, she limits what a single trade, or a single bad day, can cost you.

  • A disaster stop on every position

    No position runs without a fixed protective stop. Stops are held at the broker, so they keep working even if your computer is off.

  • 2% risk per trade

    Each position is sized so that, at its stop, it risks no more than 2% of the account.

  • 6% total open-risk cap

    Add up the risk on everything open: Rosie will not let it pass 6%.

  • Daily loss limit and kill switch

    Hit the day’s loss limit and new trading halts. No chasing losses.

  • STOP ALL and FLATTEN ALL

    One button halts Rosie. One button closes every open position. You can take over at any moment.

  • Paper mode to prove it first

    Run Rosie on a paper account with real market prices before a single real dollar is at stake.

  • A symbol without measured price history is refused, never given a guessed stop.
  • Rosie runs on your own computer. Your broker login stays with your broker.
  • Limits reduce how much a trade or a day can cost. They do not eliminate losses.
