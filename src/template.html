Section 01

What Lime Signalworks does

Lime Signalworks provides AI-assisted trading and investing support that prioritizes protecting capital before pursuing gains. Individuals and companies face the same problem: making disciplined market decisions under pressure, without outsourcing judgment to someone else's conviction.

We supply the reporting, the structure, and the decision support. We do not take positions for you, and we do not tell you what to buy. The decisions remain yours.

Section 02

Each market day we publish a plain-language summary of conditions: index-level moves, volatility, and a stated risk posture. Reports are published after the close and are labelled with their as-of date.

Section 03

What we commit to, and what we do not

We do not guarantee profits and we cannot eliminate risk. No honest provider can.

Markets move in every direction, and your decisions and results are your own. What we commit to is clear instruction, honest tooling, and a system designed to weigh capital preservation ahead of gain.

Section 04

Discipline and decision support, in one system

Lime Signalworks combines a disciplined decision process with AI assistance that reads market conditions and flags when they change. The design goal is protective: reduce avoidable losses and unforced errors first, and treat gains as the result of a process rather than the object of a prediction.

Section 05

The {{EVAL_DAYS}}-day evaluation

New users begin with a free {{EVAL_DAYS}}-day evaluation that runs in demo and paper mode only — no live capital and no real orders at any point. Access ends automatically on day {{EVAL_DAYS}}, and continuing afterwards is optional.

  1. Stage one

    Setup and orientation

    Learn what the report covers, how the dashboard is laid out, and what each condition flag means before relying on any of it.

  2. Stage two

    Daily practice

    Use the reporting and decision tools against the market each day, in demo and paper mode, and record what you would have done and why.

  3. Stage three

    Review

    Look back over the period and judge what the process did and did not change about your own behaviour and results.

The period is long enough to judge the system on your own behaviour rather than on a sales claim. Nothing in the evaluation touches a real brokerage account.

Section 06

Who this is for

This is built for people and firms that have lost money or confidence trading on impulse, and for those who want a documented process instead of a tip service.

Market mechanics are often described in language that keeps them inaccessible. We explain them plainly and show the reasoning behind each report. We make no claim about outcomes — only about the clarity of the method.

Section 10

How to begin

Self-service signup and personalized instances are designed but not yet built, so there is no account to create on this site today. No form on this page collects your name, your email address, or your phone number, because there is no provisioning pipeline yet to receive them.

Until that pipeline is built, the demonstration environment linked at the top of this page is what there is to look at. Access to it is controlled by its host rather than by us: a Perplexity sign-in may be required, so it is not anonymously reachable and we cannot promise it will open for you.

To reach a person, use the telephone number, email address, or office address in the footer. Those are the working contact paths, and contact is by appointment.
