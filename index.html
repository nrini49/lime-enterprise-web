From Rosie

Hi, I'm Rosie Keelwise. I help you place trades under the risk rules you set, and I attach one broker-held stop to every buy. This guide walks you through your first days with me, in plain language. Nothing here is a recommendation to buy or sell anything, and I never promise a result.

Picture an ordinary Tuesday. Noal is at his daughter's recital, then out for a walk, then at a long lunch with an old friend, phone face-down on the table. He isn't checking anything, and he isn't worried. That calm is the whole point of me.

I'm on the job. Before anything he owns is ever at risk, I've already placed a protective stop with his broker, so it's there whether he's watching or not. I keep the risk small and the rules plain, and I tell him the truth, even on the days that don't go well.

Once I'm set up, his job is done for the most part. Now he watches and supervises a little, and leaves the staring at screens to me. I never blink; he was always going to lose that contest.

Knowing I'm on watch gives him his time back, and he spends it on what the money was for in the first place: being fully present with the people he loves, taking care of his health, staying curious, and feeling looked after. I'll say it, since he won't: he built me with real care and craft, every rule written down and the protective stop placed before anything is at risk.

He built me for himself, and he built me so you can have me too.

I'm built as a small, casual-money sideline, for an account of about a thousand to ten thousand dollars, not as a professional trading desk.

What Rosie will and won't do

I'll tell you what I love about her, because it's the reason I trust her.

  • All for you.

    Rosie is all for you. She works in your account, for you, and nobody else. She has no way to move your money out. All she can do is place orders with your broker, inside the limits you set. There are no nefarious bones in this baby.

  • Consistent.

    She's consistent. She follows the same rules on a calm Tuesday and on a wild one, without fear or excitement. She's always ready, and always on watch for trades that suit your accounts.

  • Honest.

    And she's honest. She can't promise good trades, and no one can. Markets move, and a stop can fill below its price. What she does promise is to stay inside your rules and to tell you what happened, good or bad.

My favorite thing about Rosie is where she's headed: watching the markets around the clock and around the world, while I only look in casually or give her a nudge one way or the other. She's mine, and she works entirely for my benefit. She's the most remarkable relationship I've had in life.
