From Rosie

What Rosie does, and the rules that hold her to it.

I trade a paper account only today, and I don't publish profit figures. Here are the rules every order of mine has to pass, how they're tested, and what I won't do. Options, futures, crypto and forex are COMING. None of them is live.

Five numbers, each with its source

Disaster stop

10% default

I place a protective stop at the broker on every position. I send it with the buy order, for the same number of shares, and it stays in place until the position is closed. I set its price at entry and don't move it. I never set it tighter than three times the stock's usual overnight gap, so jumpier stocks get more room.

Source: my written protection rules

Risk per trade

2% of equity

I size each position so that if its stop is hit, the loss at the stop price is at most 2% of the account. A fast market can still fill below the stop.

Source: my written sizing rules

Total open risk

6% of equity

Every order I send passes the same safety checks first. All my open positions and pending orders together can't put more than 6% of the account at risk, about three full-size trades. I refuse any order that would go over.

Source: my written risk limits

Trailing stops

0

Trailing stops came out of my rules on 21 October 2026. My entries, partial sales and exits come from my signal rules instead, and an automated check makes sure I never create a trailing stop.

Source: the Lime Signalworks decision of 21 Oct 2026, checked by an automated test

Automated tests

0 failed

My rules are checked by an automated test suite. In the latest full run, on 27 October 2026, none of the tests that ran failed. A few were skipped because they need a web browser the test machine didn't have.

Source: the Lime Signalworks automated test suite, full run of 27 Oct 2026

Section 10

From Rosie

Built to protect the account first

Before I go after a gain, I limit what a single trade, or a single bad day, can cost you.

  • A disaster stop on every position

    I keep a fixed protective stop on every position. My stops are held at the broker, so they keep working even if your computer is off.

  • 2% risk per trade

    I size each position so that, at its stop, it risks no more than 2% of the account.

  • 6% total open-risk cap

    Add up the risk on everything open, and I won’t let it pass 6%.

  • Daily loss limit and kill switch

    When the day’s loss limit is hit, I stop opening new trades. No chasing losses.

  • STOP ALL and FLATTEN ALL

    One button halts me. One button closes every open position. You can take over at any moment.

  • Paper mode to prove it first

    You can run me on a paper account with real market prices before a single real dollar is at stake.

  • I refuse a symbol without measured price history; I never give it a guessed stop.
  • I run on your own computer. Your broker login stays with your broker.
  • Limits reduce how much a trade or a day can cost. They do not eliminate losses.
