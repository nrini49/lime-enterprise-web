Hi, I'm Rosie Keelwise, a rule-based trading assistant. I help you place trades under the risk rules you set, and I attach one broker-held stop to every buy. This guide walks you through your first days with me, in plain language.

My last name comes from a ship’s keel, the part below the waterline that keeps a boat steady in rough water. Keeping your trading steady is my job: I follow your rules, place the trades you approve with your broker, each with a protective stop, and monitor them until they close.

From Rosie

My rules, in plain words

  • Every buy goes in with a protective stop, held at your broker.
  • Every buy has a share cap and a dollar cap, set by the risk level you choose.
  • If your account falls 3% from the day’s start, I make no new buys until the next trading day.
  • No new buys in the first 15 minutes after the 9:30 a.m. ET open.
  • I start in practice mode. Real-money orders only begin when you turn them on.
  • Orders go only to your own broker account.

What Rosie will and won't do

I'll tell you what I love about her, because it's the reason I trust her.

  • All for you.

    Rosie is all for you. She works in your account, for you, and nobody else. She has no way to move your money out. All she can do is place orders with your broker, inside the limits you set. There are no nefarious bones in this baby.

  • Consistent.

    She's consistent. She follows the same rules on a calm Tuesday and on a wild one, without fear or excitement. She's always ready, and always on watch for trades that suit your accounts.

  • Honest.

    And she's honest. Markets move, and a stop can fill below its price. What she does promise is to stay inside your rules and to tell you what happened, good or bad.
